# Lukulu Academy — M02 Rhythm & Drums practice checklist

**Status:** Development prototype on `develop`; classroom pilot review still needed.  
**Target:** Adult beginner learners, FL Studio concepts through the educational DAW Lab.  
**Duration:** Approximately 45–60 minutes of instructor-led study, plus independent practice.

## Student task

1. Open the **DAW Lab** and select **Pattern A**.
2. In the **Channel Rack**, place kicks at steps **1, 5, 9 and 13**. This creates four evenly spaced beats in 4/4 time.
3. Place claps at **5 and 13** (beats two and four).
4. Add hi-hat and percussion steps to taste, checking how tempo and spaces affect the groove.
5. Make a different drum variation in **Pattern B, C or D**. You may copy an earlier pattern and change the rhythm.
6. Open the **Playlist** and assign **Pattern A** and the variation to at least one drum-lane bar each; listen with **Play arrangement**.
7. Run **Check my groove** in the M02 learning card. It reports four technical criteria below, but does **not** grade taste, mixing, authenticity, or your listening decisions.
8. Select **Export project JSON** and retain the exported file. This is a personal backup, **not a submission to a teacher**.

## What the prototype can check

| Criterion | Code checks | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Four-on-floor kick | Kick steps 1, 5, 9, 13 in Pattern A | Other kicks are allowed; groove quality is not judged |
| Backbeat clap | Clap steps 5, 13 in Pattern A | Dynamics and timbre are not checked |
| Variation | A nonempty, different B, C or D drum pattern | Does not assess musical merit |
| Playlist | Drum clips contain A and that detected variation | Does not verify actual performance or recording |

**Important:** This is a browser-only local exercise. There are no student accounts, assignment upload endpoints, grading records, teacher reviews, or cloud sync. The earlier **Mark M02 lesson complete** button remains available as an illustrative self-tracking demo; it is not equivalent to the four-check rubric.

## Teacher review

Use the future teacher review stage to assess pulse, phrasing, dynamics, arrangement choices, and originality by listening to the student's exported project or audio. Do not assign an automatic formal mark based solely on the checklist.
